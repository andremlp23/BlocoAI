## Global Inconsistencies

| ID | Category | Location / Zone | Discrepancy Found | Risk / Impact |
|----|----------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1  | Missing PDF coverage | Many BOQ trades | Pavement surfacing (concrete & asphalt), kerbs/ACO units, waterproofing details/products, joints & sealants, concrete mix acceptance and curing regimes, cladding product/fixing specifications | Technical risk: potential for contractor assumptions leading to variations and rework; financial risk: priced allowances may be optimistic/incorrect. |
| 2  | Conflicting concrete strength designations | Across BOQs | RC32/40 vs "200mm Thick Concrete 40/50 Dmax20" vs explicit C30/37, C35/45, and extra-over C35/20 sulphate resisting | Requires coordination (exposure class, durability, mix selection). Risk: mismatched specifications may produce costly on-site changes or specification of incorrect concrete. |
| 3  | Repeated / duplicated brief entries | Across zones | "Waterproofing Foundations", "Powerfloat finish", "Lean mix C12/15" | Duplication risk in take-off and double-counting labour/materials. |
| 4  | DPM / Visqueen / Radon barrier gauge mismatch | PH1 Treated Pavement, PH1 / PH4 Foundations/Floor Slabs / FSA | 1000 gauge, 1200 gauge and 15 MIL | Specification must be unified to avoid procurement/installation errors. |
| 5  | Quantities vs units suspect | Intumescent paint | 888.0 tn (same as steel tonnage) | High financial risk if accepted without correction. |
| 6  | Missing product & performance data | Waterproofing and membranes | Cetco blacksheet peel-and-stick membrane | Technical risk (waterproofing failure, warranty issues). |
| 7  | Structural scope clarity | Holding-down assembly and cast-in items | "Free issue from structural steel subcontractor" | Responsibility and supply chain coordination unclear; contractual risk if free-issue items not provided to schedule. |
| 8  | Reinforcement & mesh coordination | Multiple mesh designations | A393, A252 and mesh layer counts inconsistent | Need consolidated rebar schedule and bar bending schedules to avoid mismatch and pricing errors. |
| 9  | Joints & sawcut pattern vs floor finish requirement | Movement joint (5m x 5m centres) and SRMJ sawcut pattern | Compatibility with FM2 flatness and slab layout | Risk: joint pattern vs construction sequencing not reconciled could increase remedial works. |
| 10 | Standards coverage uneven | Structural steel and steel decking | Comprehensive PDF specs (good), but many civil/groundworks items lack equivalent contractual PDF guidance | Uneven specification risk leading to contractor-led substitutions. |

## Recommendations (Next Steps)

- Validate suspicious quantities/units (notably intumescent paint) with original BOQ authors.
- Issue clarification request (RFI) for waterproofing system(s), DPM gauge, and coordination between waterproofing and radon barrier.
- Consolidate concrete grade matrix (where C30/37 vs C35/45 vs C35/20 SR apply) and publish exposure-class-based mix schedule.
- Produce missing specification extracts or callouts for kerbs, pavements, cladding, waterproofing and joints to enable accurate pricing and execution.
