# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: Structural Steel
**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural steel to EN 10025 Grade S355JR and EN 10210 for hollow sections.
- Execution Class EXC2 per NBN EN 1090-2.
- Design per NBN EN 1990/1991/1993.
- Connections designed per NBN EN 1993-1-8 with high-strength bolted assemblies Grade 8.8 to EN 14399/EN 15048 and EN ISO 898.
- Welders qualified to EN ISO 9606-1.
- Execution tolerances and erection rules per EN 1090-2.
- Shop fabrication, connection design, shop/erection drawings, QA plan, and CE marking requirements defined.
- Holding down assemblies (H.D.A.) coordinated to structural steel baseplate requirements.
- Embedded steel plates, angles, and inserts for fixing handrails, louvres, and AOV frames.
- Earthing mats and pigtails, radon measures, and bitumen coatings at steel interfaces.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: Composite Decking
**Baseline Scope:**
- Deck sheets galvanized steel S350 per NBN EN 10346 and NBN EN 10143.
- Wide rib roof deck with no stiffening ribs on top surface.
- Installation with specified bearing, laps, closures, and puddle weld or mechanical fastening spacings.
- Headed shear studs to EN ISO 13918 welded through deck to primary steel.
- Prohibition on unapproved openings and burning.
- Alternative mechanical fasteners allowed with diaphragm design calculations.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: Fire Protection
**Baseline Scope:**
- Reactive intumescent systems to EN 16623, EN 13381-4, and EN 13501.
- Design to required fire resistance by section size and DFT tables.
- Application to blast-cleaned steel (Sa 2½) with compatible primers/topcoats.
- Detailed DFT measurement, curing, and QA rules.
- ASFP Yellow Book and TGD 11 installation practice.

## 4. TRADE PACKAGE: Corrosion Protection
**Baseline Scope:**
- Corrosion protection per NBN EN ISO 12944 (C2 internal, C4 external) and ISO 9223.
- Shop grey rust-inhibitive primers and inorganic zinc-rich primers for external members.
- Blast cleaning to Sa 2½ with roughness and dust control.
- DFT control per EN ISO 2808.
- Reflective light-coloured finish for external steel to NBN EN 1991-1-5.
