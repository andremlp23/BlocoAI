# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: Structural Steel
**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural steel S355JR to NBN EN 10025; hollow sections to NBN EN 10210.
- Execution to EXC2 per NBN EN 1090‑2.
- Design per NBN EN 1990/1991/1993.
- High‑strength bolts to NBN EN 1090, NBN EN 14399, and NBN EN 15048.
- Welding to EN 1011 and NBN EN ISO 9606‑1 with electrodes per NBN EN 1993‑1‑8.
- Fabrication and erection tolerances and connection rules per NBN EN 1090.
- Anchor bolts to NBN EN 15048; bolts, nuts, and washers to NBN EN 1090, EN 14399, and EN ISO 898.
- Grout under base plates non‑shrink, non‑metallic.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: Composite Decking
**Baseline Scope:**
- Steel deck units from galvanized sheet to NBN EN 10346 and NBN EN 10143, steel grade S350.

- Installation bearing and end‑lap requirements.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: Fire Protection
**Baseline Scope:**
- Intumescent systems based on Sherwin Williams FIRETEX FX2003 or FX6002 with C69 primer and M71V2 topcoat.
- DFT determined to achieve required fire resistance per EN 13381‑4 and EN 13501.
- Surface preparation to Sa 2.5 (EN ISO 8501‑1) with profile to EN ISO 8503‑2.
- Application and curing conditions and DFT control per EN ISO 12944‑5 and ASFP guidance.

## 4. TRADE PACKAGE: Corrosion Protection

- Internal primary steel normally C2, external metal fabrications C4.
- Shop primer and zinc‑rich primers per EN ISO 12944‑1/‑2/‑5 and EN ISO 2808.
- External handrails, stairs, ramps and exposed metal fabrications pre‑finished or galvanized to EN ISO 1461 for C4 environments.
- Blast cleaning to Sa 2.5 with surface profile and dust control to EN ISO 8501‑1/8502‑3/8503‑1/‑2.
