## Global Inconsistencies

| ID | Category | Location / Zone | Discrepancy Found | Risk / Impact |
|----|----------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Fire protection rating inconsistent | PH1 S03-A2000, PH2/PH3/PH4 | BOQ entries alternate between 1‑hour and 2‑hour intumescent requirements for structural steel across phases/zones | Incorrect coating thickness DFT, cost and procurement errors, and noncompliance with EOR fire strategy |
| 2 | Fragmented / inconsistent H.D.A. and anchor scheduling | Multiple zones | Multiple cast‑in H.D.A. quantities are split across many BOQ lines, vary between phases, and mix “free‑issue” vs “supply” without a consolidated HDA schedule | Double counting or missed anchors; interface/discrepancy with structural subcontractor & concrete contractor; potential rework and delay |
| 3 | Surface protection / finish gaps | Various zones | Some BOQ items specify galvanising + pre‑finish for C4; others only provide touch‑up allowances | Inconsistent corrosion protection, warranty and service life impacts; potential for costly touch‑up scope and remedial works |
| 4 | Decking installation & diaphragm fastenings not tied to quantities | Various zones | PDF requires diaphragm calculations and specific puddle weld/self‑tapping screw spacings; BOQ lists decking areas but does not bind fastening patterns or number of studs/welds | Insufficient fasteners / stud counts in BOQ pricing; structural/noncompliance risk |
| 5 | Fireproofing / galvanising sequencing unclear | Various zones | Where both galvanising and intumescent coatings are required, sequencing and compatibility are not resolved in BOQ | Incompatible finishes specified; increased cost/time to rework or change coatings |
| 6 | Missing manufacturer / product & test acceptance references | Various zones | BOQ lacks specific product manufacturers, DFT values for paints/intumescents, adhesive systems for chemical anchors, and testing acceptance criteria referenced in PDF | Procurement of non-compliant products, variation claims and quality disputes |
| 7 | Welders / QA / submittals and recycled content not enforced in BOQ lines | Various zones | PDF mandates welder certification, shop/erection drawings, QA, and 70% recycled steel content; BOQ items generally do not reference required submittals, certification evidence, or recycled content verification | Contractual non‑conformance and potential commercial claims |
| 8 | Anchor & bolt grade ambiguity | Various zones | Multiple BOQ entries reference bolts “as specified” but do not state bolt grades (EN ISO 898 grade numbers) consistently despite PDFs requiring Grade 8.8 / NBN EN 14399 compliance | Incorrect bolt procurement and failed on-site tests |
| 9 | Quantity discrepancies across phases for the same items | Various zones | Significant variations in tonnage and counts for identical named elements (e.g., Cooling Tower Gantry tonnages and H.D.A. counts vary PH1–PH4; decking areas differ PH2/PH3/PH4) | Scope confusion, contract change orders, pricing errors |
