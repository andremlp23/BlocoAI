# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: STRUCTURAL STEEL
**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural steel in Grade S355JR to NBN EN 10025 and NBN EN 10210.
- Execution Class EXC2 to NBN EN 1090-2.
- Design to NBN EN 1990/1991/1993.
- Fasteners to NBN EN 14399 and NBN EN 15048.
- Fabrication, connections, tolerances, and erection to NBN EN 1090.
- Welding materials and qualifications to NBN EN 1090 and EN ISO 9606-1.
