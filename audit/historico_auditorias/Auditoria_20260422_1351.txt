## Global Inconsistencies

| ID | Category | Location / Zone | Discrepancy Found | Risk / Impact |
|----|----------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1  | Missing BOQ data | All | All BOQ extracts failed (RateLimitError). Quantities and item descriptions are unavailable | Major pricing, procurement and scheduling risk |
| 2  | DFT / Fire rating unknowns | All | Intumescent systems require section-specific DFT tables and AE-provided fire-rating designations (not provided) | Financial and compliance risk |
| 3  | Potential coating/primer sequencing conflict | All | Shop painting schedule (inorganic zinc-rich primers, shop coats, galvanising exceptions) vs specified intumescent primer/topcoat (Sherwin-Williams C69, FX2003/FX6002, M71V2) | Risk of rework, warranty gaps, and schedule delay |
| 4  | Corrosion protection vs fire protection coordination | All | Multiple documents call for verification of compatibility between corrosion protection systems and intumescent coatings | Specification non-conformance or additional testing/consultant time |
| 5  | Execution class, CE marking & manufacturer qualifications | All | EXC2 plus CE accreditation and manufacturer local-technical presence will limit fabricator pool | Likely increase cost/time for vendor prequalification |
| 6  | Material sourcing constraints | All | Requirement for 70% recycled content for steel plus specific grades (S355JR, S350 for decking) | May constrain suppliers or incur premium pricing |
| 7  | Welding/site constraints | All | Where welding is restricted (site restrictions) the specs allow concealed couplings subject to A/E review | Potential for on-site change orders if restrictions apply or weld access is limited |
| 8  | Testing & QA burden | All | Extensive independent testing, batch tracking, DFT surveys, field QC per 01 40 00 and manufacturer requirements | Increase cost and programme durations; budgets/time should allow for third-party testing and remedial works |
| 9  | Design information gaps | All | Several items defer to AE (e.g., steel section vs DFT tables, durability range selections, connection grades as specified in design documents) | Pending AE inputs create scope ambiguity and price risk |

- Re-extract BOQ immediately; suspend procurement/pricing until BOQ available.
- Request AE to provide: fire-resistance schedules per member/section, required DFT per section, and specified anchor/bolt grades where noted "as specified."
