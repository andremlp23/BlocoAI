GLOBAL INCONSISTENCIES:
- Corrosion protection for primary structural steel frames (DCH PH1–PH4, FSA frame, SSA frame) is not explicitly defined in BOQ while specs require systems per NBN EN ISO 12944 (C2/C4) and EN ISO 1461 for external members.  
- Corrosion protection of envelope secondary steel (all phases and zones) and louvre/door support steels is largely absent from BOQ but mandated in specs for C4 external environments.  
- Cooling tower gantry and RMU/battery support steel in all phases are specified in BOQ as galvanized only (no intumescent) while the specification defines comprehensive intumescent systems for structural steel without phase/structure-specific limitation; reliance on fire strategy is implicit rather than explicit in BOQ.  
- Roof decking and accessories are listed in BOQ without explicit reference to the detailed fastening patterns, opening control and closure requirements that are mandated in the metal decking specification (SCI/BCSA and EN-based rules).  
- Miscellaneous stairs, ladders, railings, bollards and gratings in BOQ lack explicit performance criteria (loads, deflection, slip resistance, spacing) which are fully defined in the metal stairs and fabrications sections of the specification.
