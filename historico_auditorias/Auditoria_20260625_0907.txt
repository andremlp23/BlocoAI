# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: Structural Steel
**Baseline Scope:**
- Primary structural steel frame to EXC2, S355 in accordance with EN 1090 and NSSSBC 5th Edition.
- Structural steel members in S355JR to NBN EN 10025 / EN 10210; execution class EXC2 to NBN EN 1090‑2.
- Connection design per NBN EN 1993 and NBN EN 1991; high‑strength bolting to NBN EN 14399 / EN 15048.
- Welding materials and welders qualified to NBN EN 1090, NBN EN 1993‑1‑8, EN 1011 and NBN EN ISO 9606‑1.
- Fabrication and erection tolerances to NBN EN 1090‑2; grout at baseplates to be non‑shrink, non‑metallic with 45° splayed finish.
- Pre‑erection surveys and QA/QC, shop drawings, connection calculations and quality plan required.
