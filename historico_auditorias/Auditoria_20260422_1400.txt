# Executive Summary

## Phase: PH3
### Zone: S03-A2000

- **Structural Steel:**
  - **BOQ:** DCH Structural Steel H.D.A.
  - **Specs:** Sitewide structural steel spec (05 12 00): Steel to NBN EN 10025 Grade S355JR; Execution Class 2 (EXC2) per NBN EN 1090-2; contractor to provide connection design, pre-erection conference, shop drawings, anchor bolt checks, temporary bracing, tolerance limits (max variation 6 mm).
  - **Assessment:** Missing detail — BOQ identifies "DCH Structural Steel H.D.A." but does not state steel grade, confirmation of EXC2, shop-drawing scope or anchor-bolt coordination references required by the PDF. Needs explicit confirmation that DCH = material/finish consistent with EN standards.

- **Intumescent Fireproofing / Paint:**
  - **BOQ:** DCH Intumescent Paint 1 Hr
  - **Specs:** Sitewide fireproofing (07 81 23): Intumescent coatings to achieve specified FRR; specific products referenced (Sherwin Williams FIRETEX FX2003, FX6002, C69 primer, M71V2 topcoat); surface prep per EN ISO 8501-1; DFT/wet-film/dry-film measurement, batch numbers, environmental controls, QA testing and records; ASFP guidance (TGD 11).
  - **Assessment:** Missing detail / Partial match — BOQ specifies 1‑hour rating only. PDFs require product selection, DFTs, surface prep, and QA records. Must confirm which product/system, required DFT, compatibility with shop/field coatings and any galvanised substrates.

- **External Stairs (Ground to Roof level):**
  - **BOQ:** External Stair (Ground to Roof level) DCH
  - **Specs:** Sitewide metal stairs & ramps (05 51 00 / 05 50 00): Provide anchors, plates, hangers and attachments; pre-finish for C4 external environment if exposed; weld/site joints per EN 1011; welding certification; shop drawings and connection details required.
  - **Assessment:** Missing detail — BOQ names the stair but lacks material grade, finish, handrail/anchorage details, corrosion class, manufacturing/erection standards and whether supplied galvanized or intumescent-coated.

### Zone: S02-A7200

- **Structural Steel:**
  - **BOQ:** Steel fabricated to exc class 2, all steel grade S355. Steel erected in accordance with the NSSSBC 5th Edition.
  - **Specs:** Sitewide structural steel (05 12 00): EXC2 per NBN EN 1090-2; S355JR per NBN EN 10025; erection & temporary bracing, shop drawings, camber, alignment and anchor-bolt confirmation.
  - **Assessment:** Partial match / Potential conflict — BOQ specifies EXC2 and S355 which aligns with PDFs; however BOQ cites "NSSSBC 5th Edition" for erection while PDFs require compliance with NBN EN 1090 and local best practice. Confirm which erection standard governs (NSSSBC vs EN-based requirements).

- **Surface Treatment / Galvanising / Blast:**
  - **BOQ:** Shotblast, galvanised to EN ISO 1461
  - **Specs:** Sitewide: Hot-dip galvanise to EN ISO 1461; shop painting timing and environmental conditions; surface prep standards (EN ISO 8501-1), profile (EN ISO 8503-2); compatibility requirement between corrosion protection and intumescent systems.
  - **Assessment:** Partial match / Missing detail — EN ISO 1461 referenced matches PDF. Clarify sequence: shotblasting prior to galvanising is unusual (shotblast typically precedes painting, not hot-dip galvanising). Need confirmation of intended sequence and compatibility with intumescent/topcoat systems.

- **Stairs (Gantry Emergency Stairs; Cooling Tower Stairs):**
  - **BOQ:** Gantry Emergency Stairs; Cooling Tower Stairs
  - **Specs:** Metal stairs & ramps (05 51 00): Design, anchors, welding, C4 pre-finish for external, weld certification, shop drawings.
  - **Assessment:** Missing detail — BOQ lists stair types but lacks material grade, finish (galvanised vs painted vs intumescent), handrail specification and anchor details. PDFs supply requirements that must be referenced.

- **Railings / Handrails:**
  - **BOQ:** Railings
  - **Specs:** Sitewide: Provide wall brackets, end closures, flanges, anchors for handrails; pre-finish for C4 external environment; bracket spacing/clearance; anchor types for concrete/masonry (drilled-in expansion anchors).
  - **Assessment:** Missing detail — BOQ generic. Need confirmation of handrail material, finish class, anchorage type, and coordination with concrete/masonry.

- **Grating:**
  - **BOQ:** 30mm open mesh grating
  - **Specs:** Metal fabrications (05 50 00): general metal fabrication requirements, anchors, finishes; specific grating sizes not detailed in PDFs extract.
  - **Assessment:** Missing detail — BOQ specifies mesh size but PDFs do not confirm grating specification (load rating, material grade, finish). Require structural/load spec and finish.

- **Holding-down / Anchor Bolt Assemblies:**
  - **BOQ:** Supply Holding down bolt assemblies - MCP; Supply Holding down bolt assemblies - USS; Supply Holding down bolt assemblies - S25
  - **Specs:** Sitewide: Anchor Bolts per NBN EN 15048; anchor plates and embed placement coordination; verify grades as specified.
  - **Assessment:** Partial match / Missing detail — BOQ lists types (MCP, USS, S25) but does not give sizes, embed plate details, grades or installation tolerances. PDFs demand NBN EN 15048 compliance and anchor-bolt verification prior to erection — provide sizes/grades/plate details and installation drawings.
