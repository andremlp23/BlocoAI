# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: Structural Steel
**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural steel to NBN EN 1090 / EN 1090‑2 Execution Class 2; material generally S355JR to NBN EN 10025 and NBN EN 10210; hollow sections and plates per EN 10025/EN 10210.
- Bolts grade 8.8 to EN ISO 898 with assemblies per NBN EN 14399 / EN 15048.
- Design per NBN EN 1990/1991/1993, subgrade selection per NBN EN 1993‑1‑10.
- Fabrication/erection tolerances and workmanship per EN 1090‑2.
- Connection design and documentation rules, including reversible load checks.
- Shop fabrication, high‑strength bolting, and erection procedures including survey of anchors and temporary stability.
- Anchor bolts to NBN EN 15048.
- Erection procedures including anchor verification, bracing and splicing restrictions.
- Responsibility for connection design and documentation.
- Erection survey and correction requirements prior to steel installation.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: Composite Decking
**Baseline Scope:**
- Steel deck from galvanized sheet to NBN EN 10346 / NBN EN 10143, grade S350; wide‑rib roof deck without top stiffening ribs.
- Accessory edge/closure strips, pour stops and sump pans with specified minimum thicknesses.
- Mechanical fasteners and sidelap connectors subject to diaphragm design.
- Detailed installation rules on bearing, laps, cutting, openings, closures, welding of composite and non‑composite deck.

## 4. TRADE PACKAGE: Corrosion Protection
**Baseline Scope:**
- Corrosion protection per NBN EN ISO 12944 (C2 internal, C4 external).
- Blast cleaning to Sa 2.5 per EN ISO 8501‑1 with roughness per EN ISO 8503.
- Shop rust‑inhibitive or inorganic zinc‑rich primers with controlled DFT.
- Hot‑dip galvanizing to EN ISO 1461 with design guidance per EN ISO 14713‑1.
- External metal fabrications to be factory prefinished to C4 level with bright, light finish.
- Touch‑up of galvanized areas per EN ISO 17463.

| PH1 | MYD | Corrosion classifications C2 internal and C4 external per EN ISO 12944‑2. | None |
