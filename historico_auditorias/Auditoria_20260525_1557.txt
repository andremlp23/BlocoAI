## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: STRUCTURAL STEEL

**Baseline Scope:**
- Primary structural steel frames (FSA, DCH PH1–PH4, SSA, gantries, MYD steel, EYD trenches/interfaces) to NBN EN 1090-2 Execution Class EXC2, generally grade S355 / S355JR to EN 10025; hollow sections to EN 10210.
- Design to Eurocodes: NBN EN 1990/1991/1993 (incl. 1993-1-3 for cold-formed elements where structural, and 1993‑1‑8 for connections); supporting diaphragm/decking, cladding, louvre, canopy, cat ladder, stair and equipment loads.
- High-strength bolting to NBN EN 14399 and non-preloaded bolts to EN 15048; bolt materials typically Grade 8.8 to EN ISO 898.
- Welding procedures and welders qualified to NBN EN ISO 9606‑1, EN ISO 14732; welding execution to EN 1011 and EN 1993‑1‑8.
- Fabrication, connection design, tolerances and erection governed by EN 1090‑2, including temporary bracing, alignment, camber, and erection sequencing.
- Holding down bolt assemblies (H.D.A.) included wherever frames or gantries bear on concrete slabs/foundations; anchor bolts supplied/installed to EN 15048 with setting-out and pre-erection verification procedures.
- Secondary and miscellaneous structural steel (cladding rails, parapet steel, support frames, envelope steel, trench steels, foundations interfaces) to EN 10025/EN 10210 with same EXC2 regime as primary frames.
- Integration/interface steel: penetrations and supports for canopies and cat ladders, smoke extract louvres supports, downpipe supports, fire barrier supports, Rockspan cladding supports, movement-joint steel, RMU/battery support frames, generator/USS trench steel, gantry interfaces over MYD, and equipment support frames.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: COMPOSITE DECKING

**Baseline Scope:**
- Steel roof decking generally TATA D60 (DCH/FSA/MYD/gantry roofs) or KINGSPAN RD 100/825 (SSA Load Bank Garage roof), spanning between primary steel.
- Decking as cold-formed galvanized steel sheet from S350 to NBN EN 10346 with dimensional tolerances to NBN EN 10143.
- Structural design to NBN EN 1993‑1‑3 and relevant SCI/BCSA decking guidance; account for composite action where shear studs to EN ISO 13918 are specified.
- Installation tolerances and fastening patterns to “Metal Decking Installation”: minimum 50 mm bearing and end laps, puddle welds / self-drilling screws per specified patterns, treatment of side laps, closures, trimming, and restrictions on cutting and openings.
- Fasteners may be proprietary, subject to diaphragm design and pull‑out/shear capacity; diaphragm action required for lateral stability.
- Co-ordination for penetrations, upstands, roof openings and interfaces with cladding, plant supports and access steel.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: FIRE PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Intumescent paint systems to provide specified fire resistance periods (typically 2 hours to FSA and DCH PH1, 1 hour to DCH PH2‑PH4, 60 minutes to EYD trenches, 1 hour or 2 hours as stated per zone).
- Systems such as Sherwin Williams FIRETEX FX series or approved equivalents, tested to EN 13381‑4 and classified to EN 13501.
- Design of dry film thickness (DFT) relative to steel section factor and required rating, with tabulated thickness schedules.
- Primers and topcoats, and overall coating systems to comply with EN ISO 12944‑5 and relevant durability categories.
- Application either in factory or on site, with environmental controls for temperature, humidity, ventilation and curing; DFT measurement and acceptance to EN ISO 2808 and project “Intumescent Fire Protection Systems” specification.
- Factory-applied systems require on-site touch-up after erection to maintain continuity of fire rating at connections and damaged areas.

## 4. TRADE PACKAGE: CORROSION PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Corrosion categories generally C2 internal and C4 external for primary and secondary steel, per NBN EN ISO 12944‑2 and ISO 9223.
- Protective paint and coating systems to EN ISO 12944‑5 with defined DFT and durability class; QA/QC checks and DFT verification per EN ISO 2808.
- Surface preparation typically Sa 2½ to EN ISO 8501‑1; surface profile/roughness and dust contamination to EN ISO 8503 / EN ISO 8502.
- Galvanizing for many external steel elements to EN ISO 1461, with design guidance from EN ISO 14713‑1 (venting/draining) and touch‑up of damaged areas using compatible systems tested per EN ISO 17463.
- For galvanized external steel in C3–C4 environments, additional coating systems may be required to meet design life; factory finishing of external metal fabrications to C4 classification, aligned with project “Factory Finishing for External Metal Fabrications”.

## 5. TRADE PACKAGE: METAL FABRICATIONS (STAIRS / RAILINGS / GRATINGS)

**Baseline Scope:**
- Internal and external stairs, ramps, handrails, guardrails, balustrades, bollards, crash protection, fence posts, cat ladders, parapet stairs and small roof stairs to “Stairs, Ramps, Handrails and Guards” and “Metal Fabrications, Ladders, Barrier Rails and Miscellaneous Supports” specifications.
- Materials generally to EN 10025, EN 10210 and ISO 4200 (for hollow sections/pipes), with any bar gratings to NAAMM MBG 531/532.
- Fixed ladders and access systems to EN ISO 14122 (parts 1–4) and EN 516 for roof access; guards and rails to the stair/guard specification including loading, geometry, deflection and safety requirements.
- External assemblies (stairs, ladders, handrails, grating walkways, bumper rails, bollards, downpipe guards, parapet cappings, shutters, canopies supports) galvanized to EN ISO 1461 and factory finished for C4 environment where exposed.
- Open mesh gratings (often 30 mm) for walkways and platforms; anti-slip treatments and performance including EN 13036‑4 slip resistance where applicable.
- Miscellaneous supports and frames for louvres, downpipes, canopies, cat ladders, movement joints and barriers integrated with structural/secondary steel.

| Phase | Zone | Scope Description | Deviation / Note |
|-------|------|-------------------|------------------|
