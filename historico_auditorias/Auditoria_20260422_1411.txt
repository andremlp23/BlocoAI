# Executive Summary

## Phase: PH1
### Zone: CSA

- **Structural Steel:**
  - **BOQ:** Steel fabricated to execution class 2; steel grade S355; erection per NSSSBC 5th Edition; holding-down bolt assemblies to be cast into reinforced concrete; External Stair, Cat Ladder & balustrade (items listed); Secondary steel for cladding 3693.0 m2.
  - **Specs:** Execution Class 2 (NBN EN 1090); material S355JR (NBN EN 10025); shop drawings, welders’ certificates, CE marking, QA plan and connection calculations required; grouting of base plates with non-shrink grout (>=35 MPa).
  - **Assessment:** Match — BOQ requirement for EXC2 / S355 aligns with sitewide specs. Conflict/Action: BOQ references NSSSBC 5th Edition (local code) while the PDF specs mandate EN/NBN standards — confirm governing standard (EN vs NSSSBC) and precedence.

- **Steel Decking:**
  - **BOQ:** Supply & Install TATA D60 x 1.2mm roof decking; DCH Steel Decking reported as 7,224.0 m2 (PH1 BOQ line).
  - **Specs:** Composite roof deck; sheet material per EN 10346 / NBN EN 10143 (steel grade S350 unless agreed otherwise); minimum bearing on supporting steel 50 mm; min end laps 50 mm; stud shear connectors per BS EN ISO 13918; puddle weld and stud weld spacing/requirements detailed.
  - **Assessment:** Partial match. Missing detail / Action: BOQ specifies manufacturer/model (TATA D60x1.2) — confirm compliance with PDF product grades/gauges and provide diaphragm/slab calculations. Verify stud type/spacing and minimum bearing details in supplier submittal.

- **Intumescent Fire Protection:**
  - **BOQ:** Intumescent paint 2 hr (PH1 entry).
  - **Specs:** Intumescent coatings to achieve the fire resistance ratings designated for each member; DFT and application requirements; manufacturer’s nominal/average DFT controls (min 50% of nominal); shop/field application requirements.
  - **Assessment:** Conflict — BOQ shows 2 hr here but other BOQ entries (PH2/PH3/sitewide) show 1 hr or 60 minutes. PDF requires compliance to the design-specified rating but does not set a single rating. Action: Obtain the fire engineer’s schedule to confirm required FRR by member/location (resolve 1 hr vs 2 hr discrepancy).

- **Holding-down Bolts / H.D.A.:**
  - **BOQ:** Holding down bolt assemblies to be cast into reinforced concrete (multiple counts exist: supply-only counts appear elsewhere — PH1 lists cast-in items); DCH Structural Steel H.D.A. counts referenced.
  - **Specs:** Anchor bolts per NBN EN 15048; contractor to verify anchor rod locations minimum 20 working days prior to erection; survey and tolerances obligations.
  - **Assessment:** Match in intent. Missing detail / Action: Clarify supply vs supply+install responsibility on each HDBA line; confirm bolt type (headed vs non-headed), embedment, tolerance band and grouting/specification for grout and pocketing.

- **Connections & Composite Interfaces:**
  - **BOQ:** Load shear connector details; ConiBar to tie slabs to ground beams; construction/contraction/movement joint details referenced.
  - **Specs:** Connections designed to NBN EN 1993; shear studs requirements (min yield 350 N/mm2, min tensile 450 N/mm2); bolted connections guidance (Grade 8.8, slip-critical for moment/bracing).
  - **Assessment:** Match. Missing detail: Exact stud type/length/spacing, ConiBar specification and dowel/dowel-epoxy requirements (lap lengths) must be confirmed prior to procurement/fixings layout.

- **Concrete Interface / Forming for Steel Stanchions:**
  - **BOQ:** Forming concrete stub column to surround steel stanchion from underside of slab to top of pad foundation; casting in of H.D.A. (free issue) and grout in place (BP1/BP2/BP4).
  - **Specs:** Grout: non-shrink, non-metallic aggregate, >=35 MPa at 28 days; placement and tolerances required; contractor responsibility to verify embedded locations.
  - **Assessment:** Match. Missing detail: Tolerances for stanchion pocket, required clearances for shop-applied coatings, and sequence for grouting vs touch-up.

- **Corrosion Protection / Galvanising:**
  - **BOQ:** (No significant PH1 galvanising qty listed explicitly in PH1 BOQ extracts; galvanising quantities shown in other phases.)
  - **Specs:** Corrosion categories NBN EN ISO 12944 (internal C2, external C4); all galvanising to NBN EN ISO 1461; surface prep Sa2.5 before blasting.
