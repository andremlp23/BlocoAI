# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: STRUCTURAL STEEL

**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural design and execution in accordance with NBN EN 1990, EN 1991, EN 1993 and NBN EN 1090/NBN EN 1090-2.
- Material grades generally S355JR for rolled sections to NBN EN 10025 and hollow sections to NBN EN 10210.
- Execution Class EXC2 for fabrication and erection, including tolerances, QA/QC, CE marking and mill certification.
- Connection design and detailing including robustness; bolting to NBN EN 14399 (high-strength preloaded) and NBN EN 15048 (non-preloaded) with mechanical properties per EN ISO 898.
- Welding materials, procedure qualification and welder qualification to EN 1011, NBN EN ISO 9606‑1 and NBN EN 1993‑1‑8.
- Structural steel erection tolerances, procedures and pre‑erection surveys/checks for anchor positions per NBN EN 1090‑2.
- Anchor bolts/hold-down assemblies and baseplate grout systems (non-shrink, non-metallic aggregate) including alignment and survey requirements.
- Miscellaneous and secondary structural steel to EN 10025/EN 10210 (ledge/shelf angles, downspout guards, supports for openings, parapets, walkways, cladding support, gantry framing).
- Cooling Tower Gantry and MYD gantry/structural supports to EN 10025/EN 10210, EXC2, with connection design to NBN EN 1993.
- Roof steel supporting deck, parapets, walkways and lightning protection; roof openings and access structures coordinated with deck installation rules.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: FIRE PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Intumescent fire protection systems for primary and relevant secondary structural steel designed using EN 13381‑4 fire tests to achieve specified fire resistance ratings (typically 60, 90, 120 minutes as described).
- Fire classification and performance determined per EN 13501.
- Systems predominantly based on proprietary Sherwin Williams FIRETEX products (e.g., FX2003, FX6002) or equivalent, with defined primer, basecoat and sealer thicknesses.
- Design via DFT (dry film thickness) tables, including separate tables for different member sizes and fire durations; ability to adjust systems for varying ratings (1‑hour, 2‑hour, etc.).
- Application requirements: surface preparation (typically Sa 2.5), compatible primers (including over galvanizing where required), environmental/application conditions, and curing times.
- QA/QC regime including in-process and final DFT measurements, installer qualifications, independent verification where called for, and coordination with corrosion protection/priming.

## 4. TRADE PACKAGE: CORROSION PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Corrosion environment classification generally C2 for internal primary steel, C4 for external steel and external fabrications, to NBN EN ISO 12944‑2 and ISO 9223.
- Coating systems (primer, intermediate, finish) designed to NBN EN ISO 12944 with specified durability ranges and total DFT, including DFT measurement to NBN EN ISO 2808.
- Hot-dip galvanizing to EN ISO 1461 with design guidance to EN ISO 14713‑1 (venting, drainage, distortion, single-dip rules), including factory-applied C4 finishes where specified.
- Surface preparation for painted systems to EN ISO 8501/8502/8503 as applicable.
- Integration/compatibility between corrosion protection systems and intumescent fire protection, including compatible primers (especially on galvanized steel) and areas excluded from shop painting where intumescent will be applied.
- Touch-up and repair of damaged galvanizing and coatings in accordance with EN ISO 17463.
