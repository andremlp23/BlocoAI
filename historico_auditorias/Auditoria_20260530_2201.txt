# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: STRUCTURAL STEEL
**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural steel to Execution Class 2 (EXC2) per NBN EN 1090-2.
- Steel sections generally S355JR to NBN EN 10025; hollow sections to NBN EN 10210.
- Connection design per NBN EN 1993 and NBN EN 1991 robustness.
- High-strength bolts grade 8.8 per EN ISO 898 and NBN EN 14399/15048.
- Welding per EN 1011, NBN EN ISO 9606-1, NBN EN ISO 14732, and NBN EN 1993-1-8 Group 5.
- Execution tolerances per EN 1090; detailing and execution rules for connections, erection, temporary works, and tolerances.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: COMPOSITE DECKING
**Baseline Scope:**
- Cold-formed deck steel grade S350 to NBN EN 10346 / NBN EN 10143.
- Deck to be wide rib units without stiffening ribs in top surface.
- Detailed rules for steel deck installation, bearing, laps, cutting, openings, closures, weld and mechanical fixing patterns.
