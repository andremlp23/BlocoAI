## Recommended Immediate Actions
- Clarify governing standards (NSSSBC vs NBN EN) and confirm precedence.
- Resolve FRR requirements per zone/element (confirm 1 hr vs 2 hr locations).
- Request product submittals for all branded items and confirm equivalency.
- Reconcile duplicate zones/phase allocations and ambiguous qty lines.
- Issue RFIs for roofing specification, diaphragm calculations, fastening system design requirements, and list of mandatory QA/testing deliverables for pricing inclusion.
