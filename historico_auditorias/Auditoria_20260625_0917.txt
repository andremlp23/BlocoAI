# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: Structural Steel
**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural sections and plates to NBN EN 10025 S355JR and hollow sections to NBN EN 10210.
- Design per NBN EN 1990/1991/1993.
- Execution to NBN EN 1090/NBN EN 1090‑2, EXC2.
- Fabrication and connections per EN 1993‑1‑8, EN 1011, EN ISO 9606‑1/14732.
- High‑strength bolting per EN 14399/EN 15048/EN ISO 898.
- Holding down bolt assemblies/H.D.A. for steel supports.
- Erection survey and tolerances.
- Grout at baseplates non‑shrink, non‑metallic per grout provisions.
- Earthing/pigtails at bases.
- Secondary/AHU‑related support steel included within same execution rules.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: Fire Protection
**Baseline Scope:**
- Intumescent systems (e.g. Sherwin‑Williams FIRETEX FX2003/FX6002) with compatible primer (e.g. C69) and top sealer (e.g. M71V2).
- Systems tested to EN 13381‑4 and classified to EN 13501.
- Design and dry film thickness (DFT) per ASFP Yellow Book/TGD 11 and EN 16623.
- Fire resistance duration as per structural fire design (no fixed limit in specification).
- Compatible alkyd or other specified primer where intumescent is applied.
- Detailed DFT survey and acceptance criteria forming part of QA/QC.
- Application and curing requirements integrated with corrosion protection system.
