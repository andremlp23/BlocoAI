# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: Structural Steel
**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural steel members to NBN EN 10025 Grade S355JR and hollow sections to EN 10210 S355JR.
- Execution class EXC2 per NBN EN 1090-2.
- Design per NBN EN 1990/1991/1993 and joints per NBN EN 1993-1-8.
- Non-preloaded and high-strength bolting per NBN EN 15048 and NBN EN 14399 with Grade 8.8 bolts.
- Erection tolerances, bracing, and handling per EXC2 rules.
- Grout under baseplates non-shrink, non-metallic, installed solidly and splayed 45°.
- Lightning protection grids fixed to steel-supported roof systems.
- Welding to EN 1090, EN 1011, EN ISO 9606-1, EN ISO 14732 with stud welding to EN ISO 13918.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: Composite Decking
**Baseline Scope:**
- Steel deck units from galvanized sheet to NBN EN 10346 and EN 10143, steel grade S350.

- Accessory metals (pour stops, edges) and closures with minimum thickness requirements.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: Fire Protection
**Baseline Scope:**
- Thin-film intumescent systems for internal steel (C1) based on Sherwin Williams FIRETEX FX2003 or FX6002 with C69 primer and M71V2 topcoat.
- Systems complying with EN 16623, EN 13381, and EN 13501.
- DFT tables required per section size to achieve specified fire resistance (including 2-hour rating).
- Surface preparation to Sa 2.5 where applied direct to steel.
- Coordination with corrosion classification (C2/C3/C4) and EN ISO 12944-5.
- Application rules (ambient conditions, curing, multi-coat system, DFT control, QA/field testing).
