# Executive Summary

## Phase: PH3

### Zone: S03-A2000

- **Structural Steel:**
  - **BOQ:** "DCH Structural Steel"; "DCH Structural Steel H.D.A."; "Steel fabricated to exc class 2, all steel grade S355. Steel erected in accordance with the NSSSBC 5th Edition"
  - **Specs:** Execution Class 2 (EXC2) required (NBN EN 1090-2); steel to NBN EN 10025 Grade S355JR; erection to EN 1090 tolerances; contractor responsible for connection design/detailing; welding and bolting standards (NBN EN 1090, EN 14399, EN 15048, EN 1011)
  - **Assessment:** Match (BOQ calls for S355 and EXC2 where present).

- **Holding Down Bolts (H.D.A. / Anchor Bolts):**
  - **BOQ:** "Holding down bolt Assemblies etc"; "DCH Structural Steel H.D.A." (supply/assembly referenced)
  - **Specs:** Anchor bolts to NBN EN 15048; contractor to survey anchor rod locations 20 working days prior to erection; furnish inserts and anchorage devices where required.
  - **Assessment:** Missing detail (BOQ lists assemblies but lacks anchor bolt Grade/specification, quantities, and responsibility for survey/installation vs. supply).

- **Intumescent Fire Protection / Painting:**
  - **BOQ:** "INTUMESCENT FIRE PROTECTION"; "DCH Intumescent Paint 1 Hr"
  - **Specs:** Intumescent coatings required for primary/secondary steel to achieve specified fire ratings; specified product references (Sherwin Williams FIRETEX FX2003, FX6002, C69 primer, M71V2 sealer) and DFT requirements; primers/top coats per EN ISO 12944; surface preparation to EN ISO 8501-1; QA: wet film & DFT checks, batch numbers, application conditions.
  - **Assessment:** Partial Match / Missing detail (BOQ identifies 1‑hr intumescent but does not specify product, required DFT, sequencing with corrosion protection, or QA acceptance criteria).

- **Stairs (External Stair):**
  - **BOQ:** "External Stair (Ground to Roof level) DCH"
  - **Specs:** Metal stairs and ramps spec requires fabrication to support 500 kg/m² live load and specified concentrated loads; handrails/anchors, grating and finishes per spec; galvanizing/finish for external C4 environment.
  - **Assessment:** Match in principle / Missing detail (BOQ identifies external stair but lacks design/load confirmation, finish level (C4) and grating type).

## Global Inconsistencies

| ID | Category | Location / Zone | Discrepancy Found | Risk / Impact |
|----|----------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1  | Steel grade inconsistency risk | Sitewide | BOQ structural steel repeatedly calls out S355 (good), but steel decking spec references Grade S350 for deck sheets. These are different nominal grades — confirm structural acceptance and whether decking profile/manufacturer complies with project design loads and compatibility with S355 primary members. | Potential technical and cost risk if decking spec requires S350 but BOQ-proposed deck is different (or if thickness/profile differs). |
| 2  | Decking thickness vs spec requirements | Sitewide | BOQ: TATA D60x1.2mm roof decking (1.2 mm sheet thickness). Spec: Minimum detailing like 1.6 mm slab edge pour stops and design expectations; structural design criteria and edge-stop thickness may assume heavier sections. | Risk of non-conformance or need for supplemental sections/edge trims (cost/time impact). |
| 3  | Intumescent system and corrosion protection sequencing / product specificity | Sitewide | BOQ: generic "intumescent / DCH Intumescent Paint 1 Hr". Specs: Require specific primers, intumescent products (Sherwin Williams listed), DFTs, surface prep and compatibility verification. | Lack of specified product/DFT in BOQ creates a high QA and reinstatement risk (rework if incompatible with galvanising or shop coatings). |
| 4  | Holding down bolt scope and specification missing | Sitewide | BOQ lists multiple HDB types (MCP, USS, S25) but provides no grades, embedment, tolerances, or installation responsibility. Specs require EN 15048 anchor bolts and pre-erection surveys. | Absence of this clarity is a critical coordination and cost risk (templates, grout/inserts, tolerance discrepancies). |
| 5  | Lack of quantities, responsibilities and acceptance criteria in BOQ extracts | Sitewide | BOQ lines present items but no quantities, no clear delineation of supply vs supply-and-install in several items (HDB, intumescent works, stairs). | This creates commercial risk (priced items may exclude necessary work) and technical risk during procurement. |
| 6  | Finish/environment class not consistently specified in BOQ | Sitewide | Specs require external handrails/stairs pre-finished for C4 environment (EN ISO 12944-2), galvanising to EN ISO 1461, and zinc-coated fasteners. BOQ does not consistently reference environmental class or fastener finishes. | Risk for premature corrosion and rework. |
| 7  | QA and certification gaps | Sitewide | Welding, stud welding and applicator certification requirements (EN 1090, welder certification, applicator method statements) are specified in PDFs but absent in BOQ items. | Contractor price and programme may omit certified resources leading to non-conformance. |

### Recommendations

- Clarify and confirm steel grades for decks vs primary steel; issue engineering confirmation or revise BOQ.
- Confirm and publish required intumescent product(s), DFTs, primer/topcoat sequencing and compatibility with galvanising; incorporate into BOQ line items and allow for QA testing costs.
- Provide detailed HDB schedules (types, grades, embedment details), indicate supply vs supply-and-install, and include survey/adjustment allowances in BOQ.
- Verify deck profile (TATA D60x1.2) meets structural and edge-stop requirements; if not, update BOQ or drawings.
- Append certification and QA obligations into BOQ (welders, applicators, DFT testing, records) to prevent scope/price gaps.
