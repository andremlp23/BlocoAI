## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: STRUCTURAL STEEL

**Baseline Scope:**
- EXC2 structural steel in S355/S355JR to EN 10025 / EN 10210, executed to NBN EN 1090‑2.
- Primary frames (columns, beams, bracing) and integrated platforms/gantries, including MYD gantries, Cooling Tower Gantries, trench frames, RMU/battery supports, SSA building frames, envelope secondary steel and cladding/roof secondary steel.
- Secondary structural/miscellaneous steel to support cladding, parapets, penetrations, canopies, roof openings and access systems, detailed per NBN EN 1993.
- Bolted connections using non‑preloaded and preloaded assemblies to NBN EN 15048 and NBN EN 14399 / EN ISO 898, with bearing vs slip‑critical rules.
- Welded connections to EN 1011; no flame‑cut holes; camber/bracing control; anchor bolt checking and erection tolerances per EN 1090 and NSSSBC 5th Edition where referenced.
- Formal connection design and Quality Plan regime, including shop drawings and structural calculations where required (e.g., decks, roof openings).
- Hot‑dip galvanizing to EN ISO 1461 with EN ISO 14713‑1 design guidance (venting, drainage, distortion control) for gantries, external frames, ladders and miscellaneous supports.
- Holding‑down bolt assemblies free‑issued/supply‑only by the steel contractor for multiple foundations, to be cast‑in by civils under defined interface rules.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: COMPOSITE DECKING

- Galvanized S350 steel roof decks (wide‑rib, no top stiffening ribs) to NBN EN 10346 / NBN EN 10143.
- Composite action included where specified, with shear studs to EN ISO 13918 welded through deck.
- Standard installation rules: bearing lengths, end laps, side‑lap fixing, puddle weld patterns/screw patterns, edge closures, cutting limitations and controls around openings; no unapproved field cutting of primary deck ribs.
- Supplementary framing, bearing plates/angles and detailing around penetrations coordinated with structural steel and metal fabrications.
- Full design and shop drawings with structural calculations for deck systems, including compatibility with composite top‑deck or proprietary deck profiles that meet specified properties.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: FIRE PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Thin‑film intumescent fire protection systems (e.g., FX2003, FX6002) for structural steel, with primers and sealers defined.
- Fire resistance ratings varying by location: typically 2 h or 1 h for building frames; 60‑minute for trench steel.
- Systems tested/assessed to EN 16623 / EN 13381‑4 and classified per EN 13501, including DFT schedules per specific section factors and rating.
- Surface preparation to Sa 2.5 where intumescent applied direct to steel; compatibility with shop primer and corrosion systems (C1–C4) per EN ISO 12944‑5 and ASFP.
- QA regime for environmental conditions, application process, dry film thickness surveys and acceptance criteria.
- Factory/off‑site vs site application allowed where coordinated with sequence; intumescent not applied to decking itself and only applied to secondary steel where required by fire strategy.

**Scope Variations / Inclusions:**

## 4. TRADE PACKAGE: CORROSION PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Corrosion classifications: internal generally C1/C2; external and exposed elements C4 per NBN EN ISO 12944‑1/‑2 and ISO 9223.
- Painted systems: rust‑inhibitive or zinc‑rich primers and multi‑coat C2/C3/C4 systems; DFT checked to NBN EN ISO 2808; surface preparation to EN ISO 850x series; environmental limits on humidity and dew point during application.
- Hot‑dip galvanizing to EN ISO 1461 with design guidance to EN ISO 14713‑1; venting, drainage and distortion‑control rules for large frames and complex fabrications.
- Compatibility of primers, galvanizing and intumescent systems per EN ISO 12944‑5 and EN ISO 17463; defined touch‑up protocols for abraded galvanized surfaces and post‑erection priming/repairs.
- Factory C4 pre‑finished systems for external stairs, handrails, walkways, envelope steel and external fabrications.

## 5. TRADE PACKAGE: METAL FABRICATIONS (STAIRS / RAILINGS / GRATINGS)

**Baseline Scope:**
- Stairs, ramps, ladders, handrails, guards and barrier rails to EN ISO 14122 with defined geometry, load criteria and clearances.
- Stair construction using rolled‑channel stringers, pan or bar‑grating/chequer‑plate treads, nose requirements and joint finish criteria.
- Bar‑grating walkways/treads to NAAMM MBG 531 / MBG 532 with minimum bearing‑bar depth, open‑mesh configurations and slip‑resistant nosings.
- Fixed ladders galvanized after fabrication, with rung spacing, landings and cages per EN ISO 14122.
- Miscellaneous fabrications: downpipe guards, canopy supports, roof opening frames, edge‑confinement angles, bumper/bollard rails, fence posts, cat ladder supports and miscellaneous supports from EN 10025/EN 10210/ISO 10799 sections and plates.
- Fabrication/installation tolerances, concealed fixings where possible, continuous welds to EN 1011 and defined finish/galvanizing/C4 painting requirements.

## GLOBAL INCONSISTENCIES (ESTIMATING RISK REGISTER)

| ID | Trade Package | Issue | Risk | Estimating Action |
|----|---------------|-------|------|-------------------|
| 1 | Fire Protection | Several locations state “(See Fire Protection)” or “intumescent optional / only where indicated on drawings” without explicit ratings (e.g., SSA building, MYD supports, some secondary steel). | High | Clarify fire strategy and drawings to confirm which members are to be fire‑protected and to what rating; carry provisional sums or allowances by area/type pending confirmation. |
| 2 | Structural Steel / Metal Fabrications | Boundary between “structural steel” and “metal fabrications” (e.g., roof opening framing, canopy supports, secondary envelope steel) is blurred and described under both headings. | Medium | Agree a responsibility matrix with design team/GC showing which items are priced under structural vs metals; avoid double counting or omissions. |

| 4 | Corrosion Protection | Fire protection and corrosion protection are closely inter‑linked (e.g., factory intumescent over shop primer or galvanized steel) but compatibility responsibilities are not fully allocated. | Medium | Confirm whether steel fabricator or intumescent applicator is responsible for compatibility testing and remedial works; include allowance for additional prep/primers where applied over galvanizing. |
| 5 | Metal Fabrications | Trench zones (EYD Generator/USS) mention only “general provisions” for guards and fabrications without quantifying any specific stairs/rails/gratings. | Low | Seek trench layout details to confirm whether any guards, ladders or gratings are actually required; otherwise treat as excluded or provisional. |

| 8 | Corrosion Protection / Environment Class | Internal environment sometimes referenced as C1 and elsewhere as C2 for similar building interiors. | Low | Agree project‑wide environment classification per zone (C1 vs C2) with designer; adjust paint system build‑ups and rates accordingly. |
