# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: Structural Steel
**Baseline Scope:**
- Fabrication and erection to Execution Class 2 (EXC2).
- Steel grade S355 to NBN EN 10025 S355JR and NBN EN 10210 S355JR.
- Connection design and detailing per NBN EN 1990, NBN EN 1991, NBN EN 1993, EN 1993-1-8.
- Bolts: Grade 8.8 to NBN EN 1090, EN ISO 898, NBN EN 14399 (preloaded) or NBN EN 15048 (non-preloaded).
- Anchor bolts to NBN EN 15048.
- Welding materials and welders to NBN EN 1090, EN 1011, NBN EN ISO 9606-1, NBN EN ISO 14732.
- Grout for baseplates: non-shrink, non-metallic.
- Fabrication and erection rules, tolerances, and temporary bracing.
- Secondary steel for envelope, roof parapets, and interfaces with cooling/plant structural elements.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: Composite Decking

- TATA D60 and Euroclad/Kingspan roof deck systems.
