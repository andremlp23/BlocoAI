# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: STRUCTURAL STEEL

**Baseline Scope:**
- Structural steel generally grade S355/S355JR to EN/NBN EN 10025 and hollow sections to NBN EN 10210.
- Design to Eurocodes including NBN EN 1990/1991/1993 (and EN 1993-1-10, EN 1993-1-8 where stated).
- Execution to EN 1090-2, Execution Class EXC2; NSSSBC 5th Edition referenced for primary frames.
- Connection design, erection tolerances and QA regime defined across primary frames, secondary steel, and interfaces.
- Bolting: non-preloaded bolts to NBN EN 15048; high-strength preloaded assemblies to NBN EN 14399 where required.
- Welding to EN 1011 and NBN EN ISO 9606-1 with associated structural detailing and inspection requirements.
- Scope includes:
  - Primary frames, beams, columns, baseplates and holding-down assemblies (H.D.A.).
  - Secondary steel for cladding, parapet cappings, flashings, louvres, canopies, doors/windows, roof liners, roof penetrations, movement joints.
  - Steel supports for Eurobond/Euroclad/Kingspan roof and wall systems and diaphragm action of roof decks.
  - Generator and USS trench beams/frames and similar embedded steel.
  - Cooling tower gantry frames (Shell A) across Phases 1–4 including integration between phases.
  - Structural supports for RMUs, battery boxes, plant, AHUs, and tray/pipe support interfaces.
  - External access steel (stairs, ladders, walkways, bumper rails) designed in conjunction with EN ISO 14122.
  - Miscellaneous structural frames for roller shutters, doors, smoke extract louvres, AOVs, and openings.
  - Steel interfaces (kickers, bolts, H.D.A.) cast into foundations, pad foundations, plinths and slabs across all phases.
- Anchor bolts, confinement angles and grout requirements consistently defined for all interfaces to foundations and slabs, with survey/QA procedures for H.D.A. setting-out.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: COMPOSITE DECKING

**Baseline Scope:**
- Steel deck sheets in galvanized S350 to NBN EN 10346 and NBN EN 10143, or equivalent recognized roof deck materials.
- Design of deck to NBN EN 1993-1-3 and SCI/BCSA deck design guidance.
- Installation requirements covering:
  - Bearing lengths, end laps, side laps, and fixings/welds (including stud welding where applicable).
  - Opening formation, local strengthening and limitations on additional cuts/openings (engineer approval where noted).
  - Integration with roof systems (Euroclad, Kingspan RD 100/825 and similar) and coordination with penetrations and roof build-up.
- Use of deck as roof decking and structural diaphragm, with diaphragm design guidance where stated.
- Named profiles: TATA D60 for DCH/FSA roofs; Kingspan RD 100/825 for SSA roof; Euroclad roof systems over structural steel as indicated.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: FIRE PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Thin-film intumescent coatings to structural steel:
  - FIRETEX FX2003 or FX6002 (or equivalent systems) with performance to EN 16623, EN 13381-4 and EN 13501.
  - Fire resistance periods primarily 2 hours (R120) for major structural zones and 1 hour (R60) in later DCH phases and trenches where defined.
  - Ability to adjust fire rating via dry film thickness (DFT) in line with EN 13381-4 test data.
- Application and QA:
  - Factory or on-site spray application permitted, with on-site touch-up.
  - Blast-cleaned substrate (Sa 2.5 where specified) and compatible primer/topcoat systems.
  - Detailed DFT determination, measurement and inspection regime.
  - Coordination with corrosion protection regime and environment classification.
- Scope covers primary and selected secondary structural steel; external gantries generally exempt unless drawings specifically require.

## 4. TRADE PACKAGE: CORROSION PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Corrosion classification to NBN EN ISO 12944-2, typically:
  - C2 for internal environments.
  - C4 for external environments, including external access steel, gantries, fences, bollards, and envelope metalwork.
- Blast cleaning and surface preparation:
  - Sa 2.5 blast cleaning for intumescent-coated and painted structural steel where specified.
  - Surface cleanliness and profile control to EN ISO 8501-1, EN ISO 8502-3, EN ISO 8503-2.
- Coating systems:
  - Paint systems with DFT checks to EN ISO 2808.
  - Hot-dip galvanizing of external steel to EN ISO 1461 and EN ISO 14713-1, with venting and distortion control rules.
  - Touch-up and repair of galvanizing to EN ISO 17463 and defined site repair regimes.
  - Coordination between primers, intumescent and topcoats per environmental exposure.
