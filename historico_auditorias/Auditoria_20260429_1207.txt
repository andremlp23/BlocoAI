# Executive Report: Technical Audit Summary

## Phase: PH1

### Zone: FSA - Structural Steel
#### Subzone: FSA - Structural Steel
- **Structural Steel:**
  - **BOQ:** Design, fabrication, and erection of FSA primary structural steel frame to Execution Class 2, steel grade S355 / S355JR (711 tn), including hold down bolt assemblies (59 nr) and compliance with NSSSBC 5th Edition where stated.
  - **SPECS:** Structural steel members to S355JR to NBN EN 10025 and NBN EN 10210; execution to NBN EN 1090 / 1090-2, EXC2; design to NBN EN 1990 / 1991 / 1993; bolting per NBN EN 14399 / 15048 and EN ISO 898; welding per EN 1011, NBN EN ISO 9606-1, NBN EN ISO 14732 and NBN EN 1993-1-8; general fabrication, connection design and erection rules per Execution and Tolerances (EXC2).
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

- **Composite Decking:**
  - **BOQ:** Supply and installation of TATA D60x1.2mm steel roof decking (3 494 m2).
  - **SPECS:** Deck sheets in galvanized steel to NBN EN 10346 / 10143, grade S350; profile/installation and closures per Steel Deck Installation rules; mechanical fasteners and sidelap connectors (e.g. Hilti) and weld spacing defined; minimum bearings and laps specified.
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

- **Fire Protection:**
  - **BOQ:** Intumescent fire protection to FSA primary structural steel – 2-hour rating (711 tn).
  - **SPECS:** Thin-film intumescent systems (FX2003 or FX6002) with primer/topcoat per EN 13381-4, EN 16623 and EN ISO 12944-5; DFT tables required to achieve specified fire resistance; application and inspection regime defined (surface prep, DFT acceptance limits).
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

- **Metal Fabrications (Stairs/Railings/Gratings):**
  - **BOQ:** Miscellaneous internal steel stair in FSA; external loading dock fencing and handrails, crash protection to rising services, cat ladder, balustrades to penthouse roof, bollards, hop-over steps on roof.
  - **SPECS:** Metal fabrications, stairs, handrails and ladders to EN 10025 / 10210, ISO 4200, ISO 10799; stair and ramp components, handrails, guards, ladder components and bar gratings per dedicated fabrication/installation rules; access systems to EN ISO 14122; gratings to NAAMM MBG 531/532; galvanizing and C4 finish where external.
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

### Zone: FSA - Envelope
#### Subzone: FSA - Envelope
- **Structural Steel:**
  - **BOQ:** Secondary steel for FSA cladding support (1 942 m2) including fixing to primary steel; grouting at cladding base details; steelwork penetrations for canopies/cat ladders; interfaces with temporary protection and repair items.
  - **SPECS:** Structural and miscellaneous steel members to S355JR with EXC2 execution; secondary steel and angles/channels per NBN EN 10025/10210; connection design and detailing, and metal fabrications general tolerances governing supports for envelope.
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

- **Metal Fabrications (Stairs/Railings/Gratings):**
  - **BOQ:** Canopies, dock levellers and shelters with associated steel interfaces; aluminium downpipes, scuppers and fire barriers fixed back to steelwork; louvres and penetrations integrated with envelope steel.
  - **SPECS:** Miscellaneous steel fabrications and support steel, including downspout guards and cantilevered canopy supports, to be galvanized; fabrication and installation tolerances for metal fabrications; stair/handrail/guard rules where applicable to dock edges and access.
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

### Zone: FSA - Roof
#### Subzone: FSA - Roof
- **Composite Decking:**
  - **BOQ:** Steel deck already under Structural Steel zone; here roof build-up: Euroclad composite top-deck panels over deck (3 397 m2), roof walkways (573 m2), upstands, crickets, penetrations.
  - **SPECS:** Steel Deck Installation rules for interface with roof build-ups and penetrations; deck closures and support framing for openings; minimum bearing/end laps and fastening regime.
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

- **Fire Protection:**
  - **BOQ:** Smoke vents/AOVs installed with structural roof support interfaces; no separate roof intumescent noted beyond primary steel scope.
  - **SPECS:** Intumescent systems applicable to primary steel supporting roof deck; application and inspection regime covers members beneath roof.
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

- **Metal Fabrications (Stairs/Railings/Gratings):**
  - **BOQ:** Roof walkways and associated upstands, steel swan necks, lightning protection supports.
  - **SPECS:** Roof walkways and steps to EN 516 and EN ISO 14122; handrails/guards and possible gratings to miscellaneous steel, handrail and grating standards; metal stairs/handrails installation rules.
  - **STATUS:** ALIGNED

## GLOBAL INCONSISTENCIES

| ID | Category | Location (Phase/Zone/Subzone) | Issue | Risk |
|----|----------|-------------------------------|-------|------|
| 1  | Corrosion Protection | DCH Building Steel (All Phases) | BOQ does not define explicit EN ISO 12944-based paint systems while baseline mandates protective painting (C2 internal, C4 external). | High |
| 2  | Corrosion Protection | Envelope Secondary Steel (FSA and DCH, All Phases) | BOQ lists extensive secondary steel but no coating/galvanizing regime; baseline requires C4-level protection for external secondary/miscellaneous steel. | High |
| 3  | Corrosion Protection | External Security Fence Posts and Bollards | BOQ silent on galvanizing or protective finishes; baseline requires galvanizing/C4-rated protection for external metal fabrications. | Medium |
| 4  | Fire Protection | Cooling Tower Gantry and MYD Gantry/RMU Steel (All Phases) | BOQ consistently omits intumescent fire protection on gantry/access steel; baseline intumescent specification is generic to structural steel but does not explicitly exempt gantries, leaving a scope gap. | High |
| 5  | Metal Fabrications | Trench Steels (EYD) | BOQ includes only structural members and coatings; baseline includes broader stair/railing/grating frameworks, creating a category with baseline but no BOQ items. | Medium |
