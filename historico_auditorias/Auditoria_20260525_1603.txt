## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: STRUCTURAL STEEL

**Baseline Scope:**
- Material grade predominantly S355/S355JR to NBN EN 10025 / EN 10210.
- Design to NBN EN 1990 / NBN EN 1991 / NBN EN 1993 (incl. NBN EN 1993‑1‑8 and 1993‑1‑10 as relevant).
- Execution to NBN EN 1090‑2, Execution Class EXC2.
- Erection rules and tolerances consistent with structural steel execution requirements (NSSSBC 5th Edition where referenced).
- Bolts (high‑strength, slip‑critical and standard) to EN 14399 and EN 15048; anchor bolts to NBN EN 15048; fasteners to EN ISO 898 where cited.
- Holding down assemblies / H.D.A. and chemical anchors designed/executed to the same standards as primary steel.
- Non‑shrink grout for base plates with consistent base plate/grout interface detailing.
- Welding qualifications and procedures to NBN EN ISO 9606‑1, EN ISO 14732 and EN 1011.
- Fabrication, erection and tolerance rules consistent across all zones and phases, including secondary structural steel for cladding, louvres, canopies, ladders and access/platform steel.

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: COMPOSITE DECKING

**Baseline Scope:**
- Steel deck sheets in S350 galvanized to NBN EN 10346 / EN 10143 (including named systems such as KingSpan RD100/825 for roof areas).
- Design of cold‑formed steel deck to NBN EN 1993‑1‑3.
- Deck installation rules including minimum bearing lengths, end/side laps, cutting/notching, openings and closure details.
- Puddle welds and alternative mechanical fixings for composite and non‑composite deck.
- Stud shear connectors through deck to EN ISO 13918 with welding QA requirements.
- Prohibition of unapproved openings or thermal cutting that weakens the deck.

## 3. TRADE PACKAGE: FIRE PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Intumescent fire protection to structural steel, generally Sherwin‑Williams FIRETEX systems (e.g. FX2003/FX6002 with C69 primer and M71V2 topcoat) to EN 16623.
- Fire resistance assessment to EN 13381‑4 with classification to EN 13501.
- Use of ASFP Yellow Book and ASFP TGD 11 as design and assessment guidance.
- Dry film thickness (DFT) determination and verification to EN ISO 2808 with detailed acceptance criteria.
- Application requirements including surface preparation to Sa 2.5 (EN ISO 8501‑1) and surface profile (EN ISO 8503‑2), environmental controls and independent QA.
- Compatibility of intumescent systems with primers and corrosion protection systems (EN ISO 12944‑5).

**Scope Variations / Inclusions:**

| Phase | Zone | Scope Description | Deviation / Note |
|-------|------|-------------------|------------------|
| PH1 | FSA – Main Building | Intumescent fire protection to structural steel providing 2‑hour fire resistance using FIRETEX systems. | Higher rating (2‑hour) than several other areas; full DFT/QA detail defined. |
| PH1 | FSA – SSA Louvres / AOVs on FSA | Fire protection referenced as “See Fire Protection”. | Any protected secondary steel here to follow FSA main building baseline (2‑hour where required). |
| PH1 | DCH – PH1 | Intumescent systems and QA as per FIRETEX‑based baseline; performance by EN 13381‑4 / EN 13501; ASFP Yellow Book/TGD 11 guidance. | Rating not explicitly stated; must be confirmed at estimating stage. |
| PH1 | DCH – PH2 | FIRETEX systems with DFT selected to achieve 1‑hour rating; QA and DFT criteria aligned with baseline; compatibility per EN ISO 12944‑5/ASFP. | Clear 1‑hour requirement for this phase’s steel. |
| PH1 | DCH – PH3 | 1‑hour intumescent protection to structural steel and miscellaneous stair steel; DFT sized for 1‑hour with associated QA criteria. | Explicit inclusion of miscellaneous steel staircases in fire scope. |
| PH1 | DCH – PH4 | 1‑hour intumescent coating to structural steel and external stair steel in line with project 1‑hour requirements. | Continues 1‑hour classification and QA baseline. |
| PH1 | MYD – Trenches / Cooling Tower Interfaces PH1 | 60‑minute intumescent fire protection to generator and USS trench structural steel; factory applied with touch‑up after erection. | Factory application plus site touch‑up; 60‑minute rating. |
| PH1–PH4 | USS – Trenches / Trestles / Access Steel | 60‑minute intumescent protection to USS trench structural steel; factory applied with touch‑up. | Same rating and application method as MYD trenches. |
| PH1 | Sitewide Generator & USS Trenches | 60‑minute intumescent coating to generator and USS trench steel; factory applied with touch‑up. | Mirrors MYD/USS trench fire treatment. |
| PH1 | SSA Louvres for Smoke Extract | Fire protection referenced as “See Fire Protection”. | Any protected secondary steel to follow adjacent building system/rating (to be confirmed). |

## 4. TRADE PACKAGE: CORROSION PROTECTION

**Baseline Scope:**
- Corrosion categories C2 (internal) and C4 (external) to EN ISO 12944‑1/‑2 and ISO 9223.
- Paint systems to EN ISO 12944‑5, including shop priming, zinc‑rich primers and factory finishes for C4 environments.
- Hot‑dip galvanizing to EN ISO 1461 with design and durability guidance from EN ISO 14713‑1 (venting, distortion).
- Surface preparation to Sa 2.5 per EN ISO 8501‑1 and surface profile control per EN ISO 8503.
- DFT measurement to EN ISO 2808 and, where specified, performance evaluation to EN ISO 17463.
- “Bright, light surface” requirement for certain external steel to NBN EN 1991‑1‑5.

## 5. TRADE PACKAGE: METAL FABRICATIONS (STAIRS / RAILINGS / GRATINGS)

**Baseline Scope:**
- Materials generally to EN 10025 / EN 10210 / ISO 3574 / ISO 6317 / ISO 10799 / ISO 4200.
- Stairs, ramps, ladders, walkways, handrails and guardrails to EN ISO 14122‑1/‑2/‑3/‑4 and EN 516 for roof walkways where applicable.
- Bar gratings to NAAMM MBG 531/532.
- Slip resistance requirements to EN 13036‑4.
- Welding of fabrications to EN 1011.
- Fabrication and installation tolerances for stairs, railings, gratings, ladders, bollards, gates and fences.
- Galvanizing of internal/external fabrications to EN ISO 1461 with C4‑suitable factory finishes to EN ISO 12944‑2 / ISO 9223 for external exposure.
- QA submittals for stair/fabrication design and shop drawings where required.

## GLOBAL INCONSISTENCIES (ESTIMATING RISK REGISTER)

| ID | Trade Package | Issue | Risk | Estimating Action |
|----|---------------|-------|------|-------------------|
| 1 | Fire Protection | Fire resistance ratings vary by area (2‑hour at FSA; 1‑hour in DCH phases; 60‑minute at MYD/USS/generator trenches; some zones reference “See Fire Protection” without explicit rating). | High | Develop a location‑based fire rating matrix and confirm required rating for each frame, secondary steel and stair; clarify treatment of louvre/AOV framing and miscellaneous steel before final pricing. |
| 2 | Fire Protection | DCH PH1 fire rating not explicitly stated; only system/QA references are given. | Medium | Seek confirmation whether DCH PH1 requires 1‑hour, 2‑hour or no intumescent; carry a provisional sum or conservative allowance until clarified. |
| 3 | Structural Steel | Multiple references to existing interfaces (FSA AHU foundations, MYD/miscellaneous supports) where existing steel conditions are not detailed. | Medium | Allow for survey/measure, additional detailing and possible strengthening at interfaces; clarify extent of demolition/adjustments to existing anchors and grout. |
| 4 | Corrosion Protection | Some locations specify mandatory galvanizing (e.g. MYD/Cooling Tower PH2) while others allow paint/galvanize combinations; certain internal trench steel (Sitewide Generator & USS Trenches) is paint‑only. | Medium | Build a zone‑by‑zone corrosion schedule (C2 vs C4, galvanized vs painted) and verify with design team; avoid double‑counting both paint and galvanizing on the same elements unless clearly specified. |
| 5 | Metal Fabrications | Many zones reference “See Metal Fabrications” without explicit quantity or breakdown (stairs, ladders, guardrails, gratings, fencing, bollards). | High | Prepare an independent take‑off for all access and site metalwork (by level and zone) and validate with architectural and civil drawings; clarify which elements fall under structural vs. metal fabrications packages. |
| 6 | Composite Decking | Composite deck is referenced generically in several phases as “See Composite Decking” with no explicit area extents in this audit. | Medium | Quantify deck areas per drawing set by phase/zone and confirm system type (e.g. KingSpan vs standard S350 deck) and fire/composite requirements; check shear stud density assumptions. |
| 7 | Fire/Corrosion Compatibility | Several notes insist on compatibility between intumescent systems and primers/galvanizing (EN ISO 12944‑5), but do not define system combinations at each location. | Medium | Coordinate with coatings supplier to define approved system build‑ups (galvanized + intumescent + topcoat vs primed‑only, etc.) for each environment and rating; include cost risk for any special primers or adhesion treatments. |
| 8 | Corrosion Protection | “Bright, light surface” requirement is stated for external steel in some zones (FSA, MYD) but not all external frameworks. | Low | Confirm whether this requirement is global for all external C4 steel or only specific to named zones; if global, adjust coating specifications and allowances accordingly. |
| 9 | Structural Steel / Metal Fabrications Interface | Some secondary steel (e.g. louvre frames, curb framing, small access steel) is simultaneously tied to structural steel standards and metal fabrication tolerances. | Medium | Agree package boundaries with procurement (who supplies/measures what), and ensure no gaps or overlaps in pricing between the structural and metal fabrications trades. |
