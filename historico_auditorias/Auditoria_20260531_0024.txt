# Cross-Document Technical Audit:

## 1. TRADE PACKAGE: Structural Steel
**Baseline Scope:**
- Primary structural steel frame to Execution Class 2 (EXC2) with S355 grade in accordance with NSSSBC 5th Edition, EN 1090 and NBN EN 1090‑2 EXC2.
- Structural steel members to NBN EN 10025 / EN 10210 Grade S355JR with subgrade selection per NBN EN 1993‑1‑10.
- Design to NBN EN 1990/1991/1993.
- High‑strength and standard bolts to NBN EN 14399, NBN EN 15048 and EN ISO 898.
- Anchor bolts to NBN EN 15048.
- Welding materials and procedures to NBN EN 1090, NBN EN 1993‑1‑8 Group 5 and EN 1011.
- Fabrication and erection rules for EXC2 steel including bolting, welding, connection design, tolerances and quality plans.
- Structural interfaces at plant trestles and AHU foundations including steel hold‑down assemblies and baseplate grouting.
- Galvanized finish and touch‑up for specific structural elements.

**Scope Variations / Inclusions:**
| Phase | Zone | Scope Description | Deviation / Note |
|-------|------|-------------------|------------------|
| PH1 | FSA | Primary structural steel frame to EXC2 S355 with galvanized finish and touch‑up | None |
| PH1 | DCH | Structural steel frame to EXC2 S355; integration of roof structure with future phases | None |
| PH2 | MYD | Trench‑crossing structural steel supports for RMUs and battery boxes with galvanizing | None |
| PH3 | EYD | Foundation slabs with steel column kickers/anchor bolts for PH3 superstructure frames | None |
| PH4 | DCH | Primary structural steel frame to EXC2 S355; secondary steel for final cladding/envelope | None |

## 2. TRADE PACKAGE: Composite Decking
**Baseline Scope:**
- Steel decking specified as TATA D60 with Euroclad top deck.
- Steel deck sheets from galvanized S350 steel to NBN EN 10346 and NBN EN 10143.
- SCI/BCSA decking and stud‑welding codes referenced.
- Minimum 50 mm bearing and 50 mm end laps to roof deck.
- Detailed installation requirements including puddle welds or proprietary fasteners, sidelap connectors and treatment of openings.
- Shear studs to EN ISO 13918 with associated welding rules and temperature limits.

| PH4 | DCH | Phase 4 steel decking and Euroclad top deck with parapet upstands | None |
