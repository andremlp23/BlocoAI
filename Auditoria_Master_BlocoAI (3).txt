## Phase: PH1

### Zone: S03-A2000

- **Structural Steel:**
  - **BOQ:** FSA Structural Steel 711.0 tn; DCH Structural Steel 888.0 tn
  - **Specs:** BOQ statement: "Steel fabricated to EXC class 2, all steel grade S355; erected in accordance with NSSSBC 5th Edition." Sitewide spec applies: Execution Class 2 (NBN EN 1090-2); steel grades S355JR for angles/plates/channels/W-shapes; design and connection requirements to NBN EN 1993.
  - **Assessment:** Conflict / Ambiguity — two different main-package quantities (FSA vs DCH) reported for same Zone/Phase (risk of double-counting). EXC2 + S355 requirement from specs matches BOQ text (matching on execution class/grade), but clarification required on whether FSA and DCH are separate scopes or duplicate entries.

- **Intumescent Fire Protection:**
  - **BOQ:** FSA Intumescent Paint 2 Hr — 711.0 tn; DCH Intumescent Paint 2 Hr — 888.0 tn
  - **Specs:** Sitewide intumescent spec (07 81 23): Sherwin Williams FIRETEX FX2003 (intumescent), FIRETEX C69 blast primer, M71V2 topcoat; DFT required to achieve specified fire resistance; intumescent coatings applied to primary/secondary members.
  - **Assessment:** Conflict / Missing detail — BOQ lists fire-protection by tonnage equal to steel tonnage (units suspicious: paint measured as "tn"); PDF product and DFT requirements present but BOQ does not reference product/DFT. Also later BOQs for same zone/other phases list 1‑hr intumescent (inconsistent fire rating). Action: require confirmation of target fire rating per element and correct paint quantities/units.

- **Miscellaneous Steel Items (stairs, ladders, railing, grating):**
  - **BOQ:** Internal Stair (FSA) 1 nr; External Stair (Ground to Roof level) (DCH) 1 item; Holding Down Assemblies: FSA H.D.A. 59 nr; DCH H.D.A. 67 nr
  - **Specs:** Sitewide metal stairs & fabrications (05 51 00 / 05 50 00): stair framing, handrails/guards, metal stairs with concrete or metal treads, shop-fabrication requirements. Structural Steel Framing spec requires coordination of anchor bolts (survey prior to erection), temporary works and method statements, and shop/erection drawings.
  - **Assessment:** Missing detail / Ambiguity — FSA vs DCH duplication as above; holding-down bolt counts differ between FSA and DCH (clarify which are to be supplied/installed). Specs require anchor-bolt survey and coordination; BOQ sometimes lists "supply only" or no scope—needs scope clarification (supply vs supply+install).
